Each bloodline leaves a unique trio of evidence. Confirm signs in the field to narrow the suspects.

Goal. Identify the bloodline by gathering its 3 evidence signs, then return to the crypt and seal it before fear or the moon hits 100%.

Ten species, ten signatures. Each match picks one of ten vampires at random. Every species leaves a unique trio from a shared pool of ten evidence types — and each evidence type is read by exactly one tool. Bring a diverse loadout or coordinate with your team.

Move. WASD to walk, SHIFT to sprint, mouse to look. Click the canvas to lock the pointer. On mobile, use the joystick and drag-look.

Scan (E). Stand near a glowing clue mote with the right tool equipped, then press E. The wrong tool can't read the trace.

Switch tools (1-4). Cycle through your equipped gear with the number keys (or tap the hotbar).

Field Journal (J). Lists all ten suspects. Each confirmed evidence narrows the list — when one suspect remains you've identified the species.

Wards (Q). Burn a ward at your feet to push the vampire's threat down. Costs funds.

Seal (F). With enough evidence logged, stand on the red crypt floor and press F to banish.

Multiplayer. Host shares the room code or invite link. All hunters must Ready Up; only the host can start.
