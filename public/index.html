Goal. Each contract hides a bloodline. Find three signs, return to the crypt, and seal the coffin before fear or the moon hits 100%.

Move. WASD to walk, hold SHIFT to run. Mouse to look around. Click the canvas to lock the pointer.

Scan (E). Stand near a glowing clue mote and press E. Your loadout affects scan radius — Occultists see further.

Wards (Q). Burn a ward at your feet to push back the vampire's threat. Costs funds.

Seal (F). With all three signs logged, stand on the red crypt floor and press F.

Vampire. The longer you linger, the more the threat builds. When it hooks past the threshold it walks toward the nearest hunter. Wards burn it back.

Multiplayer. Host shares the room code or invite link. All hunters must Ready Up; only the host can start.
